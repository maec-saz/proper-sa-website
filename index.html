About Proper Developments

Building experiences, shaping destinations

Proper Developments is a Saudi-based company delivering innovative solutions across technology, construction, and project development.

Founded in 2007, Proper Developments began with a vision to deliver innovative solutions in technology and infrastructure. Over the years, the company has expanded its expertise across AV, ICT, construction, and project development — and today extends into control room solutions, AI enablement and exhibitions.

Our multidisciplinary approach ensures seamless delivery across every stage — from concept to completion — for government, enterprise and private-sector clients across the Kingdom.

Control Room Solutions

Mission-critical rooms, engineered for 24/7 operations

We design, build and support command and control environments where operators see more, decide faster and never lose the picture — combining video walls, ergonomic consoles, visualization software and resilient infrastructure into one accountable delivery.

  • Consultancy & room designOperational workflow analysis, sightlines, ergonomics, acoustics and redundancy planning to international control-room standards.
  • Video walls & visualizationFine-pitch LED and LCD walls with visualization and collaboration platforms that bring every source onto a shared operating picture.
  • Operator consoles & furniturePurpose-built technical consoles and supervisor positions designed around the operator, shift after shift.
  • KVM, signal routing & networkingSecure KVM-over-IP, signal management and resilient networks so the right operator reaches the right system instantly.
  • Support & managed servicesPreventive maintenance, remote monitoring and rapid on-site response that keep mission-critical systems running around the clock.
