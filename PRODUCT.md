## Platform

web

## Users
Buyers and evaluators of complex built and technology environments in Saudi Arabia, reading in English or Arabic:

- **Government and giga-projects:** ministries, authorities and Vision 2030 programmes procuring command and control
  centres, NOC/SOC, museums, immersive experiences and infrastructure. They need confidence in capability, scale and
  accountability before inviting a company to tender.
- **Corporate and real estate:** developers, enterprises and facility owners commissioning buildings, interior fit-out,
  audiovisual and IT systems.
- **Events and exhibitions:** organisers and agencies renting exhibition stands, LED, AV equipment and staging on deadlines.
- **Partners and suppliers:** global technology brands and subcontractors assessing PROPER as an integrator, distributor or partner.

## Product Purpose
The corporate website of PROPER Developments, a MIDRAR company headquartered in Jeddah. It presents five integrated
disciplines and its technology partners, and turns a convinced visitor into a project enquiry.

Success is a qualified **project enquiry** (contact form or email to sales@properholding.com), the primary action on every page.
WhatsApp and phone are secondary contact routes.

## Operating Context
- Visitors evaluate PROPER before shortlisting, tendering or briefing a project; many arrive from referrals, tenders or partner channels.
- Bilingual: every page exists in English and Arabic (`/ar/`, right-to-left), with a language switch and hreflang.
- Static site generated by `tools/build.py` (Python standard library), deployed to GitHub Pages; Arabic copy lives in `tools/i18n_ar.py`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Five disciplines (the site's core structure and terminology):
1. **Technology & Intelligent Infrastructure:** AI enablement, GPU solutions, robotics, command & control centres, NOC, SOC.
2. **Audiovisual & Integrated Solutions:** professional AV, LED displays, control rooms, operator consoles, integrated systems.
3. **Construction & Development:** construction, interior fit-out, infrastructure development.
4. **Museums & Immersive Experiences:** museum development, interactive exhibitions, immersive experiences, exhibition solutions.
5. **Exhibition & Equipment Rental:** exhibition infrastructure, AV equipment rental, display and technology rental.

Constraints:
- The site has no projects section (removed at the client's request). Stock or generated imagery is illustrative and never presented as PROPER work.
- Illustrative imagery must not imply PROPER built a recognisable real building or landmark.
- Pages: Home, About Us, Our Capabilities (overview and five discipline pages), Technology Partners, Contact.
- Contact form opens the visitor's email app (mailto); no form backend yet.

## Product Principles
1. Earn trust with proof, not claims: real partners and real clients only; nothing invented.
2. One accountable team: present the five disciplines as one integrated offer, not five separate businesses.
3. Every path ends in a project enquiry.
4. Equal standing for Arabic and English: neither language is a lesser translation of the other.
