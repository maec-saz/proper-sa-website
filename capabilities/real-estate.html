Overview

06Core services

We take developments from opportunity to operation — market and feasibility studies, masterplanning, development and portfolio management — shaping residential, hospitality, retail and mixed-use destinations across the Kingdom with the whole lifecycle in mind.

  1. 01

    Market & Feasibility Studies

    Market research, highest-and-best-use analysis and financial feasibility that define the right development.

  2. 02

    Masterplanning

    Land use, phasing and infrastructure strategies for communities and large mixed-use sites.

  3. 03

    Development Management

    Leading a project on the owner’s behalf, from brief and approvals to design, delivery and launch.

  4. 04

    Portfolio & Programme Management

    Coordinating several developments as one programme, with clear governance, reporting and controls.

  5. 05

    Mixed-Use & Hospitality Development

    Residential, hospitality, retail and office destinations, including operator selection for hotels.

  6. 06

    Value Engineering

    Design and cost optimisation that protects quality while improving project returns.
