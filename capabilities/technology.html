Technology & Intelligent Infrastructure

Intelligent systems for mission-critical operations.

Overview

We design, supply and integrate the compute, AI and operations infrastructure that keeps organisations informed and in control — from GPU platforms and robotics to command centres that run around the clock.

  1. 01

    AI Enablement

    Readiness assessments, use-case delivery and the data foundations needed to put AI into production.

  2. 02

    GPU Solutions

    High-performance GPU compute, storage and networking for AI training and inference — on-premise or hybrid.

  3. 03

    Robotics

    Service and delivery robotics, integrated with your facilities, systems and day-to-day operations.

  4. 04

    Command & Control Centres

    Real-time monitoring, analysis and decision-making environments for government and enterprise.

  5. 05

    Network Operations Centres (NOC)

    Purpose-built NOCs combining visualisation, monitoring platforms and resilient infrastructure.

  6. 06

    Security Operations Centres (SOC)

    SOC environments that bring analysts, processes and security tooling together in one space.
