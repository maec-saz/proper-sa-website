Who we are

One partner, from concept to completion.

PROPER Developments is a Saudi multidisciplinary company that designs, builds and integrates the environments where organisations operate, engage and grow.

Since 2007, we have grown from a technology and infrastructure integrator into a partner across six disciplines: technology and intelligent infrastructure, audiovisual systems, construction, architecture and engineering, real estate development, museums and immersive experiences, and exhibition and equipment rental.

From command centres and LED environments to buildings, destinations and brand experiences, we bring engineering discipline and creative ambition to every project, and deliver it with one accountable team: consultancy and design, supply and construction, integration and commissioning, and long-term support.

Group structure

One group. Four core domains.

PROPER Holding brings together PROPER Developments and MIDRAR, covering technology, real estate development, project management and engineering consulting.
