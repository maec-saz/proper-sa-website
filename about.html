Who we are

Integrated solutions, from concept to completion.

PROPER Developments is a Saudi company delivering innovative solutions across technology, intelligent infrastructure, construction, fit-out, digital environments and immersive experiences.

Founded in 2007, the company began with a vision to deliver innovative solutions in technology and infrastructure. Over the years, our expertise has expanded across audiovisual, ICT, construction and project development — and today spans AI enablement, command and control environments, museums and exhibition services.

Our multidisciplinary approach means one accountable team across every stage: consultancy and design, supply and construction, integration and commissioning, and long-term support.

Part of MIDRAR

Backed by MIDRAR.

PROPER Developments operates under MIDRAR, owner of the Headquarters Business Park on Jeddah’s Corniche.

The association gives our clients the confidence of an established group, and gives us a home that reflects the standards we deliver to — in architecture, technology and the day-to-day experience of the people who use the spaces we build.
